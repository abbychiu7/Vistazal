Exile in Dapitan

Though exiled to the remote town of Dapitan, Rizal transformed his isolation into an opportunity for service by establishing a school, a hospital, and a communal water system. These contributions proved that his devotion to the Filipino people remained steadfast, even while he was separated from the heart of the reform movement.
